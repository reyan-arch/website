Influencer marketing guides

Make the next campaign decision clearer.

Practical guides from IRG Media for choosing an agency, briefing creators, measuring campaigns and planning content rights across travel, hospitality, entertainment and lifestyle.

By IRG Media · Published 6 October 2026

Choose your campaign partner.

Compare influencer marketing agencies around the brief you need to deliver. Ask about creator rationale, responsibilities, costs and evidence.

Fit

Match the agency’s proposed work to your audience and goal.

Scope

Understand the inputs, decisions and delivery responsibilities.

Evidence

Separate results, planning examples and assumptions.

Read the agency selection guide ↗

Give the creator a useful brief.

Bring the audience, objective, creative opportunity and practical requirements together. Use the checklist and illustrative category examples to make open decisions visible.

Read the campaign brief guide ↗Explore the sector briefs ↗

Define the evidence before launch.

Connect the measurement plan to the decision you want to make. Separate content response, traffic and business actions. Keep attribution and reporting limits explicit.

Read the campaign measurement guide ↗Explore performance learning ↗

Agree the content use.

Record deliverables, reuse, approval ownership and disclosure checks. Resolve music, footage, access and publication dependencies before they hold up the campaign.

Read the rights and disclosure guide ↗Explore campaign operations ↗

Apply the guidance to your market.

Travel

Traveller needs, journey stages and the role of the product or destination.

Hospitality

Guest relevance, property experience, access and booking questions.

Entertainment

Audience interests, release timing, approved material and a clear next action.

Lifestyle

Recognisable routines, brand suitability and useful creator interpretation.
