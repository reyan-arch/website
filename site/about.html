Standards in practice

What we pay attention to.

Fit

The connection between creator, audience and experience. We make the selection rationale explicit so it can be discussed.

Clarity

A brief that states what matters, what is required and where the creator has room to interpret.

Ownership

A clear route for feedback and decisions, with responsibilities understood by the people involved.

Learning

Observations tied to the campaign objective, with useful recommendations and uncertainty kept visible.
