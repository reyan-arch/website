Approach

Clear roles. Confident decisions.

Frame the objective, assemble the right creators, run the campaign and carry the learning forward. Four connected stages, with a clear contribution from both IRG and your team.

01 / Frame

Make the brief a shared decision.

Before creator selection, agree the objective, audience and practical requirements. This creates a reference point for the choices that follow.

Your team

Clarifies the business objective, constraints and who can make decisions.

IRG

Translates that context into a usable brief, highlights open questions and aligns the selection criteria.

Output

An agreed brief and a clear basis for creator recommendations.

02 / Assemble

A shortlist with a reason behind every name.

Build the creator selection around relevance, suitability and the opportunity to interpret the experience credibly.

Your team

Reviews recommendations against brand priorities and flags concerns or missing context.

IRG

Evaluates fit, records the rationale and coordinates the information needed to move towards an agreed selection.

Audience

Who needs to care about this experience?

Creative context

How could the creator make it relevant?

Suitability

Does the relationship make sense for the brand?

Keep the creative direction connected to delivery.

Clear feedback and ownership help the agreed work move through the campaign.

03 / Run

Know what moves next, and who moves it.

Keep creator coordination, stakeholder feedback and approved delivery in one managed flow.

IRG prepares

Brief and requirements move to the creator.

Creator develops

Your team decides

IRG coordinates

Your team

Supplies necessary information, consolidates brand feedback and confirms approval decisions.

IRG

Coordinates creators, maintains the decision record and tracks the agreed outputs.

04 / Learn

Turn a review into a useful next step.

Read the available evidence against the objective, then decide what should inform the next campaign.

Output

A performance review with limitations, recommendations and questions to carry forward.
