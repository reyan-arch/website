Why IRG

A campaign should leave the next one smarter.

Creator marketing loses value when the relationships, decisions and learning disappear at the end of each campaign. IRG keeps that context connected.

Starting again has a cost.

A new brief should not require a new understanding of everything that came before it. Fragmented delivery can leave an in-house team rebuilding context that already existed.

Creator context

Why a creator was considered, how they interpreted the brief and where the relationship could go next. A name on a spreadsheet does not preserve that judgment.

Operational continuity

Who made a decision, what changed and what still needs attention. Without a shared record, the next campaign repeats avoidable coordination work.

Usable learning

What the available evidence suggested and which questions remained open. A final report has limited value if it cannot influence the next brief.

The difference is in what carries forward.

What continuity changes

Less reconstruction. More informed conversations.

For an in-house team, continuity means spending less of the conversation explaining what happened last time and more of it deciding what should happen next.

A clearer starting point

Existing creator context and previous decisions can inform a new brief without dictating it.

A shared reference

The team can revisit the rationale behind a recommendation, not just the final output.

Room for judgment

Learning remains specific to the objective and available evidence. It does not become a promise of guaranteed performance.
